Welcome to ShotTaker

ShotTaker runs in the background while you game and automatically captures screenshots — no platform overlay required. It saves directly to your chosen folder with organised filenames.

This wizard will walk you through configuring your setup. It only takes a few minutes.

💡 You can re-run this wizard any time from Settings → Screenshots.

📁 Browse buttons open a folder picker. If nothing happens, check your taskbar — it may have opened behind other windows.

⚠️ Performance note

Action Shots continuously monitors your screen to detect changes. On lower-end systems this may affect game performance. If you notice slowdown, switch to Time Lapse mode in Settings.

🔔 Notifications

ShotTaker can notify you when it detects a game starting.

What the notification tells you: It means ShotTaker detected an executable and started capturing. If it fires when you're not in a game, blacklist that exe from the Games tab.
